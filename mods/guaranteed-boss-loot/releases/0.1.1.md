# Guaranteed Boss Loot 0.1.1

Looting the Dead King, reaching into the Rift and examining the Dead God
always succeed without killing the searching cat. Rewards are unchanged.

Install the attached `GuaranteedBossLoot-0.1.1.zip` with Vortex or Mewtator.
Load after other boss-event mods. No DLL loader required.

[Installation and compatibility](https://github.com/anitosq/mewgenics-mods/tree/main/mods/guaranteed-boss-loot)
