# Auto Furniture listing media

Local files: `outputs/listing/auto-furniture/` (ignored).

- `00-auto-furniture-cover.png`: 1920 x 1080; primary thumbnail and gallery cover.
- `header-auto-furniture.png`: 1400 x 400; page header.
- `01-stat-targets-and-calculation.png`: stat selection, bounds and calculated results.
- `02-room-wand-buttons.png`: room buttons.
- `03-furniture-placement-mode.png`: furniture inventory and room buttons.

The three gameplay screenshots were supplied by the author on 30 September
2026, who confirmed only Auto Furniture and Mewjector were enabled. Gallery
copies are byte-identical to the supplied screenshots.

Cover and header directly combine furniture tiles from the third screenshot
with outlines from the game's Edmundm font (font ID 6 in fonts.swf). This follows
Improved Inventory's paper background, item grid and large title format. No
generated artwork or redistributed font file is used. Game artwork retains its
original copyright; see the mod's third-party notices.

Composition sources and selected glyphs remain under ignored work/; editable
SVGs and a dimensions/checksum manifest accompany the local media. Both layouts
were visually checked, including the cover reduced to 480 pixels wide.
