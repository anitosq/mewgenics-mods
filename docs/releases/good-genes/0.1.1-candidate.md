## Changes

- Identical affected left/right mutations share one comparison under the part
  group name: Arms, Legs, Eyes, Eyebrows, or Ears.
- Different current mutations retain separate comparisons. Replace Both
  clarifies the shared choice; all-limbs rolls use Replace All when multiple
  comparisons are present. Other choices retain Keep Existing/Replace.
- A single comparison has no comparison pager. Long descriptions still have
  text paging so effects remain fully readable.
- The pager has 16px clearance above and below; absent navigation reserves no
  pager space.
- Automatic occupied-slot replacements also check combined stat bonuses,
  counting identical part-group/ID bonuses once and including an unchanged
  counterpart. A locally better part can no longer bypass this guard by
  collapsing two distinct bonuses into one.
- Defined negative mutation IDs, including missing-part -2 outcomes, follow
  ordinary empty-slot/special-choice rules. Undefined negative IDs remain
  protected; valid missing-part previews are labeled Missing part.
- Breeding, native write scope, and overnight behavior are unchanged.
