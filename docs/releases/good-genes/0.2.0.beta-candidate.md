## Scope

- Stat trade-offs open the selector; clear improvements remain automatic.
- Both columns show inherited base stats plus active mutation bonuses, with
  incoming increases green and decreases red. Unchanged values retain their color.
- Descriptions use a taller panel and smaller text without text paging.
  Arrows appear only between distinct part comparisons. Descriptions exceeding
  twelve lines keep the current mutation rather than show incomplete effects.
- Matching mutation bonuses are deduplicated; inactive shapes and embedded coat
  copies do not add extra bonuses. Previewing does not mutate the live cat.
