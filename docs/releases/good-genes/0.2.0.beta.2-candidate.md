Prepared 2026-10-09 after the owner requested fixes for the 0.2.0 audit.
Preparation only; no installation, save changes or publication authorized.
The earlier beta and published 0.1.0/0.1.1 archives remain unchanged.

## Changes

- Policy and preview include facial effects hidden or restored by a head change.
  A detached native placement clip supplies the active flags without modifying
  the cat. The comparison shows affected facial parts and uses Replace Head.
- Absent paired eyes, eyebrows and ears use the game's effective -2 definition,
  counted once per pair. Single absent sides and an inactive mouth use 0.
- Later rolls wait behind an open choice, including automatic or rejected
  candidates, and are re-evaluated in order after the preceding choice.
- Packaging rejects stale source/version/commit and DLL/UI/append outputs.
  Source hashes include the shared asset generators and tools.
- The shared part collector uses one bounded appearance read. No idle-frame
  work was added; head placement projection runs only for head offers.
