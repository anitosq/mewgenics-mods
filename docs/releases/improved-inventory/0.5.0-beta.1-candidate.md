# Improved Inventory 0.5.0-beta.1 candidate

## Stage and scope

Prepared on 22 October 2026 after the owner confirmed 0.4.0-beta.6 and asked
for filters by stat bonus and by effect. It includes all 0.4.0 betas, the
Backpack probes among them. No installation, deployment, game launch or save
access was performed.

## Research

- Wiki terminology: the [Stats](https://mewgenics.wiki.gg/wiki/Stats) page
  lists seven main stats (Str, Dex, Con, Int, Cha, Spd, Lck). The
  [Keywords](https://mewgenics.wiki.gg/wiki/Keywords) page lists buffs,
  debuffs, damage over time and defensive effects.
- Supported-build item data (`data/items/*.gon`, 1,144 definitions): stats
  are top-level numbers, both bonuses and maluses. Counts: Cha 74, Spd 57,
  Con 53, Int 53, Lck 41, Str 28, Dex 22. Armor has a top-level `shield`
  (178) and sometimes `divine_shield` (15). Effects appear as identifiers in
  `passives` and nested blocks (for example
  `AddStatusToBasicAttack { Bleed 1 }`). 373 items grant an ability defined
  in `data/abilities/*item_abilities.gon`. Some effects come mostly from
  abilities, for example Stun on 6 items directly and on 13 through their
  ability.
- Chosen effects: the most frequent effects that a player would search for,
  with direct and ability counts. Bleed 20+13, Poison 14+10, Burn 10+7,
  Freeze 11+1, Stun 6+13, Bruise 19+9, Confusion 7+10, Slow 9+6, Fear 11+3,
  Weakness 3+7, Thorns 43+3, Brace 51+2, Shield 11+3 (plus armor shield),
  Holy Shield 7+1, Health Regen 42+6, Dodge Chance 23+3 and Charge 18+1.
  Knockback was dropped: it has no `KEYWORD_*_NAME` localization key.
- Every label is the game's own localized keyword name, for example
  `KEYWORD_STRUP_NAME` "Force (+)" and `KEYWORD_BLEED_NAME` "Saignement" in
  French. A missing key falls back to English.

## Implementation

- Item traits come from the effective loaded definition at item bind, so data
  merges by other mods count. The walk follows the GON object layout already
  used for sets: children `+0x38`, number `+0x58`, string `+0x68`, key
  `+0x88`, type `+0xa8`, 0xb0 bytes per node. It visits at most 512 nodes and
  8 levels and ignores `set`, `name` and `desc`. Each definition pointer is
  walked once.
- Stats: a top-level positive or negative stat field, or a direct
  `passives { <Stat>Up }`, counts as a bonus or malus.
- Effects: exact identifier matches (a table in
  `tools/generate_item_traits.py`). Armor `shield` and `divine_shield` count
  as Shield and Holy Shield. Abilities use a generated build-time table of
  83 abilities. Abilities added by other mods are not covered. The packager
  regenerates the table and fails if the committed header differs.
- UI: the search field is shorter (placeholder "Search items...") and two
  dropdowns now sit beside it. Stats selects one bonus or malus, or Any.
  Effects toggles any number of effects (match any), with Clear and Done.
  The Reset button also clears both. The filters combine with type, rarity,
  sets and search, and apply to Storage, Trash, equipment selection and the
  Backpack.

## Offline checks

- Python reader tests, browser model tests and all native fixtures pass.
  The new `native_traits` fixture covers table ordering, the predicate,
  popup geometry and a synthetic GON tree (stats, passives, armor shield,
  ignored set field, unknown ability, cache, malformed vector). The toolbar
  fixture covers dropdown labels and both popups.

## Owner test required

Use a backed-up campaign copy. At home and in the Backpack:

- Stats: pick a bonus (for example Luck (+)) and a malus. Check the shown
  items against their tooltips; then Any.
- Effects: pick Bleed, then add Poison (match any); Clear; Done. Check an
  item whose ability inflicts the effect (for example a stun weapon).
- Labels in French; the popups and dropdowns fit; hover, clicks and Escape.
- Combine with search, type, rarity and sets; Reset clears all.

Fixture results do not establish label fit in-game, popup placement or that
every item's effect appears as expected.
