## Cover prompt

Create a polished 16:9 Nexus Mods cover image for the Mewgenics mod titled exactly 'Guaranteed Boss Loot'. References 1 and 2 are the actual relevant game boss loot event illustrations: Dead King and Dead God. They MUST form the image/background, keep their recognizable exact silhouettes, faces and flat cartoon ink style. Reference 3 is ONLY typography and overall visual family reference from our Improved Inventory mod; DO NOT use any inventory icons, tiles, grids, weapons or unrelated objects from it. Compose actual boss event artwork prominently, Dead King at lower left and Dead God at lower right, on the game's light event-page paper backdrop. Keep the bosses clearly visible and not hidden under lettering, using muted original red/pink tones. Large black quirky hand-drawn native-game-style title in two lines at upper/central area: 'Guaranteed' then 'Boss Loot', readable at thumbnail size. Similar handmade restrained appearance to reference 3 but relevant boss art replaces ALL inventory background. No other text, badges, borders, fake UI, generic chests, added characters, or gradients. Landscape 16:9.

## Header prompt

Adapt the supplied Guaranteed Boss Loot cover into a polished ultra-wide 3.5:1 Nexus description header. Same paper event-page background, actual recognizable Dead King corpse on far left and Dead God corpse on far right, same restrained muted red/pink game art. Large black hand-drawn title exactly 'Guaranteed Boss Loot' in center, split two lines if needed, readable, with enough space that both bosses remain recognizable. Keep all text and illustrations safely within image. No inventory icons, grids, generic chests, badges, extra words or invented characters. Preserve the illustration design and overall visual family. Wide landscape banner about 1400x400.
